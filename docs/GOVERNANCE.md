# Governance

## Purpose

E34 Physics Laboratory is maintained as an open educational research project. Decisions should protect four things at the same time: physical accuracy, learning quality, contributor accessibility, and honest communication about uncertainty.

## Roles

The repository owner is the initial maintainer and release editor. Contributors take responsibility for the work they submit. People who make sustained, careful contributions may be invited to review within their area of experience, such as curriculum, physics, vehicle evidence, accessibility, visual design, or software.

No title allows a reviewer to approve work outside their competence without appropriate review.

## Decisions

Routine corrections and small features are decided through pull-request review. A change should be accepted when it is in scope, supported by appropriate evidence, understandable to its intended learner, tested in proportion to risk, and compatible with project licensing and safety boundaries.

Changes to the curriculum spine, mastery interpretation, learner data practices, license, safety language, vehicle evidence policy, or core simulation assumptions should begin with a public issue. The issue should record the alternatives and the reason for the decision before implementation is merged.

When reasonable contributors disagree, the maintainer will seek the clearest evidence, ask for a small test or prototype where possible, and record the decision. Decisions can be revisited when new evidence appears.

## Research and educational integrity

The project will not describe an untested intervention as proven, a visual proxy as measured geometry, or a calibrated approximation as an exact digital twin. Conflicts of interest, sponsored material, and materially generated research or educational content should be disclosed.

Research involving learners must use appropriate consent, privacy protection, and institutional review where required. Identifiable learner data does not belong in the repository.

## Releases

The maintainer prepares releases from reviewed commits on `main`. A release should have a changelog entry, passing automated checks, current status and limitation notes, preserved third-party attribution, and a version tag. Stable course claims require more than a successful software build; they need documented content review and learner evaluation.

## Conduct and security

Community behavior is governed by [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Vulnerabilities are handled through [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). These policies apply to maintainers as well as contributors.
