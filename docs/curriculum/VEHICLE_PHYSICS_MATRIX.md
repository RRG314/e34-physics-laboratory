# Vehicle physics matrix

This matrix is a planning registry. `Available` means present in the current vertical slice; `mapped` means the prerequisite and vehicle role are defined but the activity is future work.

| E34 system | Physics domains | Recurring concepts / depths | Principal prerequisites | Experiments | Diagnostics | Instrumentation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Complete car | Kinematics, dynamics, energy | Position/velocity/acceleration from intuitive through state-space and parameter estimation | Units, graphs, algebra; later calculus/ODEs/statistics | Predict-run-measure motion; future road-load estimation | Speed disagreement, acceleration anomaly | Position/time/velocity graph | Available: Level A kinematics |
| Wheel/tire | Geometry, rotation, friction, gas/thermal, materials | Circumference, `v=rω`, inertia, slip, nonlinear tire force, model fitting | Motion; later force, calculus, dynamics, statistics | Radius/speed what-if; future grip/slip/pressure | Imbalance, pressure loss, wheel-speed mismatch | Radius tool, RPM/ω telemetry; future force/slip sensors | Available through first-year rotation |
| Brakes | Force, torque, pressure, energy, heat, materials | Hydraulic force, brake torque, kinetic-energy conversion, transient temperature | Force + rotation + friction + energy + pressure | Stopping/rotor heating | Pull, fade, pressure imbalance | Pressure, temperature, wheel speed | Mapped |
| Drivetrain | Rotation, torque, power, vibration | Ratios, inertia, torsion, efficiency, coupled dynamics | Angular motion + torque + energy; later ODEs | Follow torque and speed through ratios | Speed-order vibration, slip | RPM at each shaft, torque/power flow | Mapped |
| Suspension | Dynamics, oscillation, controls, materials | Spring/damper -> quarter car -> nonlinear multi-DOF estimation | Newton's laws; later ODEs, linear algebra, signals | Bump response, damping what-if | Resonance, worn damper | Displacement, acceleration, FFT | Mapped |
| Engine | Thermodynamics, fluids, rotation, materials, controls | Pressure/work, cycles, heat, flow, combustion, crank dynamics | Energy + pressure + thermo + fluids + calculus | Cylinder-pressure work, efficiency models | Misfire, overheating, low pressure | Pressure, RPM, temperature, signals | Mapped |
| Cooling | Thermodynamics, heat transfer, fluids, controls | Heat capacity, convection, flow networks, coupled thermal states | Temperature + energy + fluids; later ODEs | Coolant-flow reduction | Thermostat/pump/radiator faults | Temperature, flow, pressure | Mapped |
| Electrical | Circuits, E&M, signals, controls | Voltage/current, resistance, induction, transients, sensing | Charge/current; alternator adds rotation + magnetism + induction | Voltage drop, starter load, induction | Battery discharge, bad ground, sensor fault | Multimeter, oscilloscope, current probe | Mapped |
| Body/glazing/lighting | Optics, waves, materials, structures | Reflection/refraction, glare, polarization, beam patterns, stress | Waves/E&M for deeper optics; materials for structure | Windshield reflection; lamp aim | Poor illumination, optical distortion | Ray/angle tools, photometer | Mapped |

The car is not forced to demonstrate every topic. Pure interference and other weak vehicle examples should begin on dedicated apparatus, then reconnect to lamps, glass, radio, or sensors only where physically defensible.
