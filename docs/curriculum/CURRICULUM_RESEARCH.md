# Curriculum research

## Method

The initial graph compares the scope and order of OpenStax University Physics with MIT's introductory and intermediate sequences. It does not copy a single syllabus. Decisions favor prerequisites shared across sources and require the learner to predict and measure before unlocking additional vehicle systems.

## Representative sources

- [OpenStax University Physics Volume 1](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/preface) sequences units and measurement, vectors, straight-line motion, multidimensional motion, Newton's laws, energy, momentum, fixed-axis rotation, angular momentum, elasticity, gravitation, fluids, oscillations, waves, and sound. It is designed around the usual two- or three-semester calculus-based sequence.
- [MIT 8.01SC Classical Mechanics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-01sc-classical-mechanics-fall-2016/pages/syllabus/) begins with kinematics, then Newton's laws and circular motion, momentum, work/energy, torque, rotation, angular momentum, and rolling. Single-variable calculus is a corequisite.
- [MIT 8.01X](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-01x-physics-i-classical-mechanics-with-an-experimental-focus-fall-2002/pages/syllabus/) places measurement standards, units, dimensions, 1D displacement/velocity/acceleration, and experimental work at the front of introductory mechanics. This supports the laboratory's prediction-measurement-assessment pattern.
- [MIT 8.02X](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-02x-physics-ii-electricity-magnetism-with-an-experimental-focus-spring-2005/pages/syllabus/) proceeds through charge, field, potential, circuits, magnetism, induction, AC/RLC behavior, Maxwell's equations, waves, polarization, interference, and diffraction after mechanics.
- [MIT 8.03SC](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-03sc-physics-iii-vibrations-and-waves-fall-2016/pages/syllabus/) requires multivariable calculus and electricity/magnetism, assumes mechanics, and adds damped/driven oscillation, resonance, modes, Fourier decomposition, waves, reflection/refraction, interference, and diffraction.
- [MIT 8.223 Classical Mechanics II](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-223-classical-mechanics-ii-january-iap-2017/pages/syllabus/) requires introductory mechanics and multivariable calculus before Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics.
- [MIT 2.003J Dynamics and Control I](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003j-dynamics-and-control-i-spring-2007/pages/syllabus/) requires introductory mechanics and differential equations before equations of motion, stability, vibration, and numerical analysis.
- [MIT 2.06 Fluid Dynamics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-06-fluid-dynamics-spring-2013/pages/syllabus/) covers pressure, hydrostatics, control volumes, conservation laws, viscous pipe flow, dimensional analysis, boundary layers, lift, and drag.

## Graph policy

Concept nodes state what is required, what they unlock, why the car is appropriate, and what the learner demonstrates. A node is not added merely because a component can be associated with it. Supplementary apparatus is preferred when the vehicle would create a misleading analogy.
