# Open E34 model research

## Selected candidate

**BMW 525i e34 | Project Zomboid** by Uralvagonzavod

- Source: <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bmw-525i-e34-project-zomboid-c65aa3b7687d4f5dbbabdfad0b7816bb>
- License reported by the official Sketchfab API: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
- Geometry reported by the API: 37,890 faces and 21,948 vertices
- Suitability: correct 525i identity, moderate real-time geometry, interior and opening-panel reference visible
- Current use: attributed 1920×1080 model thumbnail on the homepage and garage page
- Integration blocker: Sketchfab's official archive endpoint requires authentication. Do not rip the viewer or bypass access controls. Obtain the archive through the creator-authorized download flow, preserve its attribution file, inspect textures/materials, optimize to GLB, and validate scale/pivots before replacing the schematic physics mesh.

## Other candidates reviewed

- **BMW E34 [stance style]** by Myedsu — CC BY, 128,586 faces. Visually modified rather than factory-stock.
- **1995 BMW M5 (E34)** by DisneyCars — CC BY, 252,805 faces. Strong later-tier visual candidate but heavy for direct web use.
- **1995 BMW 528i (E34)** by DisneyCars — CC BY, 4,478 faces. Lightweight but not the target 525i identity.

The model used for presentation and the physics collision/measurement representation may remain separate. Visual geometry must not silently become dimensional evidence.
