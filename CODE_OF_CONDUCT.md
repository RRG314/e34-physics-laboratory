# Community code of conduct

E34 Physics Laboratory welcomes people with different backgrounds in education, science, engineering, vehicle ownership, design, and software. A useful community depends on curiosity, patience, and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking the person presenting them.
- Make room for beginners and explain specialized terms when they matter.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept correction in the same spirit.
- Credit other people's research, writing, software, measurements, and creative work.
- Respect privacy and remove personal or identifying information from shared vehicle data.
- Treat safety claims, educational claims, and uncertain evidence with appropriate care.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, plagiarism, falsified evidence, and sustained disruption are not welcome in project spaces. Publishing another person's private information or copyrighted material without permission is also unacceptable.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code. Decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and focused on protecting the community and the integrity of the project.

For an immediate safety or privacy concern, use GitHub's reporting tools. To raise a concern privately with the repository owner, contact [RRG314 through GitHub](https://github.com/RRG314). Reports will be handled as confidentially as the available channel allows.

This code applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and project-related public events or correspondence.
