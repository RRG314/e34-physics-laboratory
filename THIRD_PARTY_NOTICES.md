## BMW 525i E34 visual model and reference image

`public/models/e34-525i/e34-body.glb`, `public/models/e34-525i/e34-wheel.glb`, and `public/assets/e34-525i-reference.jpg` are adapted from or depict **BMW 525i e34 | Project Zomboid** by [Uralvagonzavod](https://sketchfab.com/UVZZZ), published on [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bmw-525i-e34-project-zomboid-c65aa3b7687d4f5dbbabdfad0b7816bb).

The source work is offered under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The model adaptation isolates one sedan, removes the source display scene and unused variants, aligns it to the laboratory axes, separates the wheel geometry for interaction, converts the selected geometry and textures to GLB, and scales it to the laboratory's documented tire-radius assumption. The preview image is used unchanged apart from responsive display and cropping by the browser.

The adapted GLB files remain under CC BY 4.0 and are not relicensed under MIT. Detailed adaptation notes are included beside the files in `public/models/e34-525i/LICENSE.md`.
